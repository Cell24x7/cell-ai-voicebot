# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how **{{company}}** ("we", "us") processes personal data when businesses use **{{appName}}** (the "Service") at {{website}}. The Service lets a business (our "customer") call and message its own contacts with AI voice agents, call flows and a shared WhatsApp inbox.

## 1. Who is responsible

For the data our customers upload or create (their contacts, calls, WhatsApp conversations, recordings and transcripts), the **customer is the Data Fiduciary** and we process that data **on the customer's behalf and only on its instructions**. For the data of the people who sign up and use the dashboard (account users), and for billing and security records, **we are the Data Fiduciary**.

Contact: {{email}} · {{address}}

## 2. Data we process

- **Account users:** name, e-mail address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), role, sign-in sessions, security and audit events, preferences.
- **Customer contacts:** name, phone number, e-mail and the custom fields the customer imports or enters (for example a loan account number or a due amount), tags, lists, do-not-call and opt-out status.
- **Calls:** phone numbers, call times and outcomes, the **audio recording** (when the customer turns recording on — a recording notice is played first), the **transcript**, keys pressed, and what the AI agent or call flow did during the call.
- **WhatsApp:** for a WhatsApp Business number the customer connects through Meta, we receive from Meta the account and phone number identifiers and status, an access token (stored encrypted), the messages, media, templates, reactions and delivery statuses of the customer's conversations, WhatsApp call events and call permission answers, and the contact's WhatsApp profile name.
- **Payments and invoices:** wallet top-ups are paid through Razorpay; we receive the payment status and reference, never full card or bank details. We keep GST invoices and wallet ledger entries.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser and device information, and service logs. Logs never contain message texts, call audio, passwords or access tokens.

## 3. Why we process it

- To provide the Service the customer asked for: place and receive calls and WhatsApp messages and calls, run its AI agents and call flows, record and transcribe calls, keep the inbox, show reports.
- To bill the prepaid wallet, issue GST invoices and prevent fraud.
- To keep the Service secure (authentication, rate limits, audit logs) and to fix problems.
- To meet legal obligations (for example tax records).

We do **not** sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use customers' contacts, calls or messages to train AI models.

## 4. Meta / WhatsApp data

We access a customer's WhatsApp Business account only after the customer connects it through Meta's Embedded Signup and only to provide the features the customer uses in the Service (numbers, templates, messages, calls, profile and billing status). We use the WhatsApp Business Platform under Meta's terms and the WhatsApp Business and Commerce policies. When the customer disconnects a number, we delete the access token and stop syncing it.

## 5. AI processing

AI voice agents, call flows with AI steps, speech recognition, speech synthesis and knowledge-base search use **OpenAI** (United States) as our processor. The audio and text of a call or a test conversation are sent to OpenAI to produce the answer or the transcript. Under our agreement OpenAI does not use this data to train its models; its own retention for abuse monitoring applies. Customers decide whether their calls use AI.

## 6. Who receives data (processors)

- **Meta Platforms** — WhatsApp Business Platform (messages, calls, templates, number management).
- **OpenAI** — AI agents, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, embeddings.
- **Razorpay** — payments.
- Our **e-mail provider** — account e-mails (verification, invitations, alerts, invoices).
- Our **hosting provider** — servers, storage and backups in India.

Each processor receives only what it needs for its part of the Service. We may disclose data when the law requires it.

## 7. International transfers

Data is stored on servers in India. OpenAI and Meta may process data outside India. We transfer data only as allowed under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

## 8. How long we keep data

- Call records, recordings and transcripts: **180 days** by default, then deleted automatically.
- WhatsApp messages and media: **90 days** by default, then deleted automatically.
- Each customer can choose shorter periods in Settings → Data retention (recordings can be deleted before the call record), and we may set a shorter limit for an account. Daily report totals, which contain no personal data, are kept for 25 months.
- Contacts: until the customer deletes them or closes its account.
- Invoices and wallet ledger: as long as tax law requires.
- Backups: up to **30 days**.
- WhatsApp webhook events: up to 30 days.

## 9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS). Access tokens, numbers' PINs and other secrets are encrypted at rest. Access inside a customer account follows roles (owner, admin, manager, agent, viewer); every sensitive change is audited. Our staff access customer data only to provide support the customer asked for.

## 10. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask for a summary of your data, its correction, completion, update or erasure, nominate another person, and raise a grievance.

- If you are a **contact** of one of our customers (for example you received a call or a WhatsApp message), please contact that business first — it decides about your data. You can also write to us and we will forward your request to the business.
- If you are an **account user**, write to {{email}}.

**Grievance Officer:** {{grievanceOfficer}} · {{email}}. We answer within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## 11. Deleting data

- A customer can delete contacts and turn off recording in the Service, and can disconnect a WhatsApp number at any time.
- To delete an account and its data, the account owner writes to {{email}} from the owner's e-mail address.
- If you connected WhatsApp through Facebook, you can remove {{appName}} in Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations. Meta then sends us a deletion request and we delete the access tokens and your Meta user id; you can check the status on our [data deletion page](/data-deletion).

## 12. Cookies and browser storage

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in (a secure, HTTP-only refresh cookie). The browser also remembers a few preferences (light / dark theme, table columns, recent emojis, ring sound). We use no advertising or analytics cookies in the application. Meta may use its own cookies during the WhatsApp connection flow, under its own notices.

## 13. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly process children's data for our own purposes.
